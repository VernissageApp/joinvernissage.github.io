Vernissage is a community-driven platform designed for professional photographers, ad-free and algorithm-free.

Key features

ActivityPub support

Connect with photographers on Mastodon, Pixelfed and other platforms through ActivityPub federation.

Photography-centric

Browse multiple timelines: photos from people you follow, trending images, hashtags, editor's picks and more.

Metadata & EXIF

Share EXIF data (camera manufacturer, model, settings) or your own information (optionally share photo location).

HDR & image quality

Display HDR images to fully take advantage of modern monitors.

AI integration

Automatically generate ALT descriptions for accessibility or suggest hashtags.

Notifications

Configure push notifications for likes, new followers and other interactions.

Account verification

Verify your account if you have a well-known personal website.

Open source project

All source code is available under the Apache 2.0 license - anyone can view and modify it.

Why Vernissage?

Decentralized

Thanks to the open ActivityPub protocol, every Vernissage server is independent yet interoperable — together we form a global community network.

No ads

Your timeline is filled with what matters to you, not what a corporate algorithm suggests.

Chronological content

You choose who to follow - posts appear in chronological order with no algorithmic manipulation.

Safety & privacy

Your data and your time are yours alone - Vernissage will never sell your data or serve ads.

Community driven

The platform is built by photography enthusiasts. You can join, contribute improvements and shape the platform's future.
